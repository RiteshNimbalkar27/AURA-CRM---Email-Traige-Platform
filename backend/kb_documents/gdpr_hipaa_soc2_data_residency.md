# Compliance, Privacy, and Data Handling Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-01

This document outlines Nexus Software Inc.'s compliance certifications, data privacy policies, and handling procedures for sensitive data.

## Certifications
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Nexus is SOC 2 Type II certified. Our annual audit report is available to Enterprise customers under an NDA.

## GDPR Compliance
- **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)**: Available for all customers. Please contact legal@nexus.example.
- **Sub-processors**: A full list of active sub-processors is available at nexus.example/sub-processors. Changes to this list are published with a 30-day notice.
- **Data Residency**: The standard data center is located in US-East (Virginia). An EU data residency option (Frankfurt, Germany) is available exclusively on the Enterprise plan.

## HIPAA Compliance
- **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)**: Available for Enterprise plan customers operating in the healthcare sector.
- A specific HIPAA-compatible account configuration is required. Contact compliance@nexus.example for setup.

## Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR)
- **Submission**: DSARs must be submitted to privacy@nexus.example with the subject line "DATA SUBJECT ACCESS REQUEST".
- **Timeline**: We respond within 30 calendar days. This may be extended to 60 days for complex requests, with prior notice given to the data subject.
- **Verification**: Identity verification is strictly required before any data is processed or released.

## Right to Deletion (GDPR Article 17)
- Submit deletion requests to privacy@nexus.example.
- Processed within 30 days.
- Note: Some data may be retained to comply with legal obligations (e.g., billing records are kept for 7 years, audit logs as defined per contract).

## Data Retention and Deletion
- **Active Customers**: Data is retained for the lifetime of the account plus 90 days post-termination.
- **Terminated Accounts**: All customer data is securely deleted within 90 days of termination, unless a legal hold applies.
- **Backups**: System backups are retained for 30 days.

## Data Breach Notification
In the event of a confirmed data breach:
- **Supervisory Authority**: Notified within 72 hours of discovery.
- **Affected Customers**: Notified without undue delay once the scope of the breach is confirmed.
- Notifications will be sent directly to the designated account owner email address on record.
