# Pricing Tiers and Discounts Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-01

This document outlines the standard pricing tiers and discount policies for Nexus Software Inc.

## Pricing Plans
- **Starter Plan**: $29/month, includes up to 5 users. Best for small teams starting out.
- **Professional Plan**: $79/month, includes up to 25 users. Includes advanced integrations and analytics.
- **Enterprise Plan**: Custom pricing, for teams with 25+ users. Includes SSO, priority support, and custom data residency. Contact sales@nexus.example for Enterprise pricing.

## Free Trial
We offer a 14-day free trial on the Professional plan. No credit card is required to start the trial.

## Billing Cycles & Annual Discounts
- Subscriptions can be billed monthly or annually.
- Customers who choose annual billing receive a 20% discount compared to the monthly rate.

## Volume Discounts
For customers purchasing larger seat counts, the following volume discounts apply:
- 10+ seats: 10% discount
- 50+ seats: 15% discount
- 100+ seats: 20% discount

## Special Discounts
- **Non-profit organizations**: 30% discount (must provide valid 501(c)(3) documentation).
- **Educational institutions**: 40% discount.

*Note: Discounts are not stackable unless explicitly defined in an Enterprise agreement. If a customer qualifies for multiple discounts, the highest applicable discount will be applied.*

## Grandfathered Pricing
Pricing is locked for 12 months from the initial contract date. Customers will not experience a price increase during their first 12 months, regardless of public pricing changes.

## Plan Modifications
- **Mid-cycle upgrades**: Upgrading your plan or adding seats mid-cycle will be prorated for the remainder of the current billing cycle.
- **Downgrades**: Downgrading a plan or removing seats will take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. No prorated refunds are issued for mid-cycle downgrades.

## Feature Differences
- **Starter**: Basic project management, standard integrations, email support.
- **Professional**: Advanced reporting, custom workflows, premium integrations, API access.
- **Enterprise**: SAML SSO, dedicated Customer Success Manager, custom API limits, SLA guarantees.
