# Refunds and Retention Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-01

This document outlines the standard policies regarding refunds, cancellations, and customer retention strategies for Nexus Software Inc.

## Refund Policy
- **Monthly Subscriptions**: 30-day money-back guarantee for new monthly subscriptions (applicable to the first subscription only).
- **Annual Subscriptions**: 30-day refund window for new annual contracts. After 30 days, cancellations are only eligible for prorated credits applied to the account (no cash refunds).
- **Month-to-Month Cancellations**: Access continues until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for the remaining days in the cycle.

*To request a refund, customers should email billing@nexus.example or initiate the process in the in-app account settings.*

## Retention Playbook
When a customer expresses intent to cancel, the following retention offers may be presented:
- **Subscription Pause**: Offer a 3-month pause (account is frozen, and data is preserved).
- **Downgrade to Starter**: Offer to downgrade the account to the free Starter plan to maintain basic access.
- **Loyalty Discount**: Offer a 20% loyalty discount for customers with a tenure of 2+ years.
- **Dedicated CSM Intervention**: For Enterprise accounts at risk of churning, assign a dedicated Customer Success Manager.
- **Extended Trial**: Offer an extended trial of new premium features they haven't yet used.

## Churn Risk Escalation
Accounts exhibiting the following behavior should be flagged for churn risk:
- More than 2 support tickets opened within a 30-day period.
- Expressing neutral or negative sentiment in communications.

## Win-Back Offers
For customers who have churned but return within 90 days, we offer a **25% discount for their first 3 months** back as a win-back incentive.

## Retention Authority Matrix
- **Support Agents**: Authorized to offer subscription pauses or plan downgrades.
- **Customer Success Managers (CSMs)**: Authorized to offer up to a 20% discount.
- **VP of Sales**: Approval required for any discounts exceeding 20%.
