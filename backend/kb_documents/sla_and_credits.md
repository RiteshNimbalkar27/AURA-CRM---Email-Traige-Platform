# Service Level Agreement (SLA) and Credits

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-01

This Service Level Agreement outlines the uptime guarantees, credit structures, and support response times for Nexus Software Inc. customers.

## Uptime SLA Guarantee
- **Professional and Enterprise Plans**: Guaranteed 99.9% monthly uptime.
- **Starter Plan**: Best-effort uptime (no SLA guarantee).

## Downtime Credit Tiers
If monthly uptime falls below our 99.9% guarantee, Professional and Enterprise customers are eligible for the following credits based on their monthly fee:
- **99.5% – 99.9% uptime**: 10% of monthly fee
- **99.0% – 99.5% uptime**: 25% of monthly fee
- **Below 99.0% uptime**: 50% of monthly fee

*Maximum allowable credit per month is 50% of the monthly fee. Credits are applied to the next invoice and cannot be redeemed for cash refunds.*

## How to Claim SLA Credits
Customers must submit a ticket to support@nexus.example within 30 days of the qualifying downtime incident to claim credits.

## Support Response Times
- **Enterprise Plan**: <1 hour response time (24/7 coverage)
- **Professional Plan**: <4 hours response time (Business hours: Mon-Fri 9AM-6PM ET)
- **Starter Plan**: <24 hours response time (Business hours only)

## Incident Communication Protocol
In the event of service degradation, our communication standards are:
- **P1 (Full outage affecting >50% users)**: Status update every 30 minutes on status.nexus.example.
- **P2 (Degraded performance, key features affected)**: Status update every 2 hours.
- **P3 (Minor issue, workaround available)**: Status update within 24 hours.

## SLA Exclusions
The following scenarios are excluded from SLA downtime calculations:
- Scheduled maintenance (provided 7-day advance notice is given).
- Customer-caused outages (e.g., API abuse, misconfiguration).
- Force majeure events (e.g., natural disasters, widespread ISP outages).

## Status Page
Real-time system status is always available at status.nexus.example.
